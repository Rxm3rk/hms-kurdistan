## Verdicts on the seven questions

| # | Question | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | SI-12: no money fact in any guard; discharge never blocked by bill, deposit or pre-approval; administrative close never blocks clinical discharge; ED registration and emergency admission never gated | **Holds for money.** No predicate of either machine reads a bill, deposit, tariff, payment, pre-approval or RCM state. Creation has no guard. All three Discharge transitions carry only a time guard (or none). AdministrativeClose comes after `discharged-not-closed`, which every clinical consumer already treats as discharged, and it reads only HIM coding and journal charge capture (CTRL-0012-3 to the letter). B-8 (elective admissions not gated by deposits) follows CTRL-0012-3. **But emergency admission IS gated by non-financial fail-closed bed checks with no override path (SAF-bed-01, critical).** |
| 2 | SI-15: which commands are refused offline; is internet loss wrongly treated as offline; is anything that must stay possible during a cell outage blocked | **The offline set is correct and stricter than §8.4, and that is put to the CSO (E-1, E-2, B-7).** Refused offline: encounter AdmitToBed, TransferBed, SwapBeds, CancelDischarge (both); bed Reserve, Occupy, Reoccupy. Allowed offline: Arrive, Triage, leave and return, all Discharges, CancelAdmission, MarkEnteredInError, Vacate, cleaning, Block, Close, CancelReservation. **Internet loss is not treated as offline:** the predicate reads the issuing mode only (on-site-cell / cell-lost / edge-agent / mobile-offline) and never WAN state, which matches SI-15's last sentence, §16.3 L1 and CTRL-0015-3. During the outage nothing that must stay possible is blocked: discharges can be recorded or back-entered with the paper time. **After the outage, though, back-entry of admissions, transfers and swaps that really happened is not reliably possible (SAF-encounter-03, major).** Channels running on the server have no defined mode (SAF-encounter-12, minor). |
| 3 | SI-17: routing pending results at discharge and at cancelled admission | **Sound as an effect that never blocks** (an effect, not a guard; pool queue plus TaskOrphaned when no owner resolves; it covers all three Discharges, CancelAdmission and MarkEnteredInError; E-4 counts final-unacknowledged results too). **Gaps:** orders with no result yet, active orders and scheduled doses, and the cancelled pre-admission are not covered (SAF-encounter-07). Wrong-patient encounters send their results to normal follow-up (SAF-encounter-06). Results are not re-routed after a cancelled discharge (SAF-encounter-14). |
| 4 | Bed B-6: can reservation failing closed on unknown gender, isolation or pressure signal block an emergency admission? | **Yes.** Reserve is the only way into a bed (AdmitToBed, TransferBed and CancelDischarge (b) all need `targetBedAssignedToEncounter`), and it has no emergency or override path. A stale building signal makes every monitored isolation room impossible to reserve, and those rooms are exactly where an airborne-isolation patient must go. **Critical (SAF-bed-01).** |
| 5 | Over-blocking at 3 a.m. | One critical (B-6). Two majors: triage needs every configured vital and fails on an empty list (SAF-encounter-09); downtime back-entry (SAF-encounter-03). Two minors: CancelDischarge privilege cover at night (SAF-encounter-14); a staffing-blocked clean bed must be "cleaned" again (SAF-bed-03). The time guard on Discharge refuses only an impossible time, but nothing can correct a wrongly timed earlier entry, so it can push users to record a false time (SAF-encounter-04). No patient is left with no way to be admitted, transferred or discharged once the bed checks are fixed: from arrived or triaged a patient can always be discharged (#10), from in-progress and on-leave always (with a later time). |
| 6 | H1 wrong patient or bed: swaps, returns after cancelled discharge, occupancy exclusion | **Returns after a cancelled discharge are sound.** Case (a) is allowed only for the last occupant, with nobody on the bed since, privileged and audited. Case (b) needs a fresh reservation. **The bed-side exclusion is sound:** half-open periods, entered-in-error periods ignored, a bed on leave counts as held, it is checked on both sides, and the swap opens and closes periods at the same instant. **Defects:** a swap skips gender, isolation and pressure checks (SAF-encounter-01). SI-01 / HAZ-0001 is never considered for ADT commands, including how the swap partner is identified (SAF-encounter-02). Nothing stops one patient occupying two beds (SAF-encounter-11). Correcting a wrong-patient admission vacates a bed that is still occupied (SAF-encounter-06). Device dissociation is missing (SAF-encounter-08). |
| 7 | Liveness / SI-03: every encounter ends (LWBS, death); every bed returns to available | **The graph is live in both machines** (format rule 6 checked by hand: every encounter state reaches `finished`, `cancelled` or `entered-in-error`; every bed state reaches `available`). LWBS, AMA, transfer out and ED discharge end through proposed #10; absconding on leave ends through #9. **Death is never mentioned**: it can only be a Discharge, and no disposition is recorded (SAF-encounter-10). A planned encounter that never arrives keeps its bed reservation with no owner or timer (SAF-encounter-16). A bed held for a patient on leave stays occupied until the patient returns or is discharged, which is intended. |
