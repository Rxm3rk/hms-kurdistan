## Checked and found sound

- **Schema and structure.** Both files validate, and every state reaches a terminal state. Every exit from a terminal state carries `privilegedAndAudited` (appointment: ReverseNoShow and `*→entered-in-error`; claim: ReopenClaim). Every `proposed` edge is used, and every undrawn *edge* is listed (four drawn appointment transitions; the 22 claim transitions treated as drawn were checked against §9.23 and R11 §10.8, and only the two in SAF-CLAIM-01 are wrong). Approval blocks are `requested`, `[]`, `null`, with the §8.4 approvers (`opd-panel`; `rcm-lead`, `kurdistan-pack-pm`).
- **Determinism.** Every alternative group is disjoint through exact complements: requiredParticipantsAccepted/participantAcceptanceAwaited, waitlistOffer/notWaitlistOffer, timely/lateCancellation, offerResponseWindowOpen/Elapsed, settledInFull/Short, enhancementApprovedBefore/noEnhancementApprovedBefore, and the four mutually exclusive `queryOn…` stage predicates (including the five-way `RecordDenial` groups from `query` and `query-response`).
- **SI-12, appointment.** No predicate or effect reads or writes a money fact. ED registration never needs an appointment. Pay-first is kept out of the machine (DN-A4, the stricter default, correctly put to the CSO and OPD panel). The phase-2 structural check (no money field in the facts) is the right test.
- **SI-12, credit claim.** All 16 effects target rcm, platform-tasks (desk queues only) or hub. "Escalation never to clinicians" is encoded in `startSlaClock` and `assignQueryToDesk`. An emergency admission is intimated after the fact. Withdrawal moves liability without touching care. The boundary is left to ArchUnit and to the cross-boundary property (CTRL-0012-3; §9.20 acceptance 4), which is correct.
- **Money.** Every amount comparison is exact and in one currency, with no conversion. Totals and balances are ledger answers. Claim-build counting is integer units only, and §2.1's "dose arithmetic" concern does not arise. Deduction, denial, exception and withdrawal reasons are governed codes, user-chosen and never defaulted.
- **SLA pairing.** Every entry into a request state starts the clock, every entry into `query` pauses it, every exit from `query` resumes or stops it, and every decision stops it. Calendar maths is left to platform-tasks (§11.7).
- **Appointment reasons and corrections.** Cancellation, no-show and correction reasons are user-chosen, never prefilled. The late-cancel flag carries no charge. There is no automatic no-show sweep. A declined or lapsed slot is not offered to the same entry again. Waitlist ranking is total and deterministic. "Left before seen" is recorded as `cancelled`, not no-show. Every ending and reversal posts to the referral. SI-15 correctly does not cover slots.
- **The §9.23 chain and its additions.** The chain is complete. The `pre-approval-denied`, `closed` and `withdrawn` additions are justified by R11 §10.8. Appeal from `settled` is correctly not offered.
