Reviewer: automated review (AI), not a person. Worktree `t3-5dcdaa39`, branch `wp/0.5-state-machines`, uncommitted work in progress. Read only. Files: `docs/specs/state-machines/medication-administration.yaml` + `.md` (MAR), `docs/specs/state-machines/order.yaml` + `.md` (ORD). **Summary: 3 critical, 16 major, 10 minor.**

## Verdicts on the integrator's seven questions

1. **Split override (product-scan vs verification).** Faithful to SI-02 and to B.3's comment, and stricter than B.3's guard (which dropped both checks). No transition skips both the verification and the product scan. **Three gaps remain:** (a) the "identified mismatch is a hard stop" claim does not hold, because `productNotIdentified` is per command, so leaving out the scan gets around it (SAF-MAR-02, critical). (b) `firstDoseOfOrder` resets after a stop, an entered-in-error dose or a replacement (SAF-MAR-04). (c) A hold and resume may undo a "returned" status (SAF-ORD-02). It also over-blocks: an emergency dose of an unmapped or unbarcoded ward-stock product before verification has no path (SAF-MAR-03, SAF-MAR-10).
2. **Wristband scan on every path.** **Not safe as the default as argued, and not put honestly to the CSO.** The md misreads B.3. B.3's own `GiveWithOverride` guard uses `twoIdentifiersConfirmed`, §8.4's example guard list names it, and HAZ-0002 expects a wristband-scan bypass *with* a recorded reason. SI-01 asks for two identifiers, not a scan. Under the default, a time-critical dose for a patient with a missing or unreadable wristband (or a wristband still showing a temporary MRN since merged) cannot be documented. Because there is no back-entry, the dose is then given without any record (SAF-MAR-03, major).
3. **Exact-equality product match.** **Not safe, and only partly honest** (SAF-MAR-01, critical; SAF-MAR-06). With "identified mismatch = hard stop, no override", the correct drug in a different pack size, strength or concentration, or a product whose linear barcode carries no expiry, can never be documented. Examples: 2 × 20 mg furosemide for a 40 mg dose; 4 mg morphine from a 10 mg/mL ampoule; a 1 g order against a product labelled 1000 mg. The claim that "pharmacy supplies it as one labelled unit dose" is not supported by §9.10. Infusions are undefined.
4. **ORD-2 re-verification on Verify, Hold, Resume, Discontinue, Mark error.** **Acceptable.** It is one confirmation, never a block: alternative (b) can always be met. Stopping the wrong patient's drug is a harm too. The DN should offer the middle option and state the delay (SAF-ORD-07, minor). The real over-blocking of stops is in `actorPrivileged`: an unknown privilege requirement or an expired licence fails closed on Hold and Discontinue (SAF-ORD-03, major).
5. **ORD-8 hard-stop rule that cannot run refuses Sign and Verify.** **Unsafe** (SAF-ORD-01, critical). It contradicts SI-05's dose-arithmetic clause (c) and CTRL-0005-7/-10: "dose check unavailable" sends the order to pharmacist review. During a KB outage or slowdown, every medication Sign is refused, emergency orders included. So is every Verify, which removes the pharmacist, the very fallback.
6. **SI-15, SI-12, SI-13, SI-07.** **SI-12:** clean in both files; no money fact is read. **SI-15:** the order side is sound. On the MAR side, `offlineAllowed` and `activeVerifiedOrder` are ambiguous about whose knowledge and which mode count, and a synced dose that fails revalidation is dropped (SAF-MAR-05). **SI-13:** the definition of independence is sound, but the check is not tied to the product scanned or to a time (SAF-MAR-12). **SI-07:** sound for Sign and Verify. The MAR has no performer predicate at all (SAF-MAR-07).
7. **Liveness / SI-03.** **Holes:** scheduled doses of held, discontinued or replaced orders cannot be closed by any MAR transition, and they raise late-dose tasks (SAF-MAR-09). Running infusions of a discontinued order get no stop task. Drafts, including cutover pre-builds, and indefinite holds have no owner or timer, although the format can express one (SAF-ORD-04). Paper doses given during downtime have no back-entry path (SAF-MAR-10).
