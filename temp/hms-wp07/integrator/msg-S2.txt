feat(i18n): WP-0.7 S2 catalogues, pseudo-locales and clinical-keys lint

Slice S2 of WP-0.7 (design system and client shells) fills
frontend/packages/i18n with the message keys of the design-system
specification and the localisation tools the later slices use.

What S2 adds:
- The catalogues: all 121 keys of docs/specs/modules/design-system.md
  §10 in en, ckb and ar. The English is the specification's, word for
  word (a test checks it against the §10 table). Every ckb and ar text is
  an agent draft (DN-021): the build agent, an LLM, wrote it in its own
  session and no text went to any other translation or LLM service.
  Each draft is marked unreviewed in src/messages/review/<locale>.json,
  and the clinician review stays a gate before hospital use (OQ-151,
  KA-031).
- src/pseudo.ts: the in-memory pseudo-locales en-XA (accented,
  bracketed, padded by 40%) and ar-XB (literal runs wrapped in
  U+202E…U+202C, direction rtl); only literal text changes, so every
  ICU placeholder is kept.
- src/ui-intl.ts: the Intl tags en-, ckb- and ar-u-ca-gregory-nu-latn,
  uiIntlLocale() and hasNonWesternArabicDigits().
- src/sorani-letters.json and .ts: the ten Sorani letters and the pairs
  ي/ی, ك/ک and ه/ە, with code points, Unicode names and whether the
  Arabic form is itself a Sorani letter (so ه and ە are never swapped
  mechanically).
- src/lint/clinical-keys.ts: under banner.* and clinical.*, a ckb or ar
  message equal to the English fails. Equality is decided on the parsed
  messages, with literals normalised (NFC; white space, format and other
  default-ignorable characters removed), so RLM, ALM, ZWSP or ICU
  spacing cannot hide an English text; a plural keeps # in every branch
  where the key is clinical or the English keeps it.
- src/lint/review-marking.ts and src/review-status.ts: the per-key
  unreviewed marking, its lint and reviewStatus(); src/lint/json-object.ts
  holds the shared JSON file checks taken out of catalogue-lint.
- Arabic redrafts after review: banner.weight.source.stated ("حسب
  الإفادة", reported, never "authorised"), sign.outcomeUnknown ("من غير
  المعروف ...") and hal.capture.discardedAfterContextChange ("تم حذف
  ..."); these and the other drafting points are listed for the reviewer
  in OQ-151.
- OQ-159 (open): the review marking format, the # rule, the
  pseudo-locale tags, the letter-pair flag and en's Intl tag.
