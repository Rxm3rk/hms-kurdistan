feat(hal-client): WP-0.7 S8 HAL client transports and label-printer simulator

Slice S8 of WP-0.7 (design system and client shells) builds the web
side of HAL contract v1 (docs/specs/hal/) and the label-printer capture
endpoint. The Tauri shell itself is slice S9; no Tauri runtime has run.

What S8 adds, in frontend/packages/hal-client:
- contract.ts: Zod schemas repeating hal-v1.schema.json for the twelve
  calls, every request, value, event and error kind, and the closed audit
  record; message-key helpers (hal.error.outcomeUnknown when the outcome
  is unknown).
- transport-tauri.ts: the IPC binding hal_call, hal_subscribe (one
  channel per subscription) and hal_release_blob (OQ-157). Every request
  is checked before it is sent (one outside the contract is refused and
  never crosses the bridge) and every reply and event after; an unusable
  reply, a rejected invoke, a no-shell reason from the shell or no reply
  within the 180 s client deadline becomes a typed error result with a
  fresh UUIDv7, never an exception. A success reply to hal.id.read
  becomes unavailable/not-implemented; to hal.pay.request, an unknown
  outcome (KA-027).
- transport-none.ts: with no shell every call returns
  unavailable/no-shell with its own audit record, and never throws, even
  with a broken clock or ID source.
- transport-sim.ts: a simulated shell that checks requests, results and
  audit records against the contract; a refused request copies nothing
  into its record; barcode reads reach only the focused window.
- capture.ts: a capture bound to the window's patient id, change counter
  and change time; a result that arrives after a context change is
  discarded with hal.capture.discardedAfterContextChange and its blob
  released. It holds the window's modal guard while it waits.
- client.ts (shell detection, no-shell otherwise) and uuid7.ts.

In tools/simulators/label_printer (Python standard library only): a raw
port-9100 capture endpoint that stores each job's ZPL, PDF or bytes with
a JSON sidecar (format, size, sha256, time), atomically. It binds to
loopback only unless --allow-non-loopback is given (with a warning),
limits connections, jobs and stored bytes, and never reuses or
overwrites a job number, across restarts or two servers on one
directory. tools/README.md describes it.

Integrator changes: docs/specs/hal/README.md names the simulator's real
path, tools/simulators/label_printer/ (a hyphenated directory is not an
importable package, so unittest discovery would not find its test).
OQ-157 (open): the IPC binding, request and reply handling, the client
deadline [P], dropped events and the capture binding. KA-040 (open): a
failure only hal-client sees has no audit record of its own.
