feat(design-system): WP-0.7 S5a patient banner

Slice S5a of WP-0.7 (design system and client shells) builds the one
patient banner of docs/specs/modules/design-system.md §2.1 in
frontend/packages/design-system/src/banner.

What S5a adds:
- PatientBanner: a <section> named banner.region.label that renders
  PatientIdentity, then allergies (NKA, not assessed, unavailable, or a
  list showing every item whole), code status, isolation and the dated
  weight with its source and date, through ClinicalNumber (U+202F group
  separators removed from ui-core's display form, character work only).
  In the error state every field still renders; the section carries
  data-error-state, data-signing-blocked and data-identity-incomplete
  from ui-core's banner model. An optional actions slot.
- PatientIdentity, reused by the switch confirmation, the
  re-verification step, the palette and the chooser: the error state,
  the photo, every name form (labelled, own script first, a missing form
  shown as missing), the MRN in an LTR bdi, the date of birth at its
  precision with the approximate flag, and "Identity incomplete".
- PatientPhoto ("No photo" when absent, invalid or failing to load),
  BannerStatus and BANNER_STATUSES (text, icon shape and tone for each
  status), StatusIcon (12 inline SVG shapes, aria-hidden, never
  mirrored, outlines distinct within a field), GovernedLabel ("Not
  translated" on fallback) and useBannerLocale.
- banner.css: logical properties and token pairs only; no ellipsis,
  line clamp, fixed height, hidden overflow or nowrap on text;
  overflow-wrap: anywhere. Imported by styles.css; the banner is
  exported from the package entry point and listed in the README
  (integrator wiring).
- OQ-161 (open): S5a's engineering choices (decorative icons named by
  their text, tones, the error-state layout, the photo placeholder, how
  ClinicalNumber gets the weight, governed-label locale, wrapping,
  reuse of PatientIdentity).
