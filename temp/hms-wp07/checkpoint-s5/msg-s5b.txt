feat(design-system): WP-0.7 S5b sign and co-sign, command palette and density control

Slice S5b of WP-0.7 builds design-system §2.3 to §2.5 in
frontend/packages/design-system/src/{sign,command-palette,density}.

What S5b adds:
- SignDialog: a view of ui-core's patient-context machine; it sends
  only the machine's events. Review, then re-verification or
  authentication, then the intent statement with Sign or Co-sign. On a
  return to re-verification (signerDiffers, server refusal) the earlier
  authentication is dropped and the signer authenticates again before
  "This is the right patient" is enabled; Confirm is disabled without a
  valid banner identity; focus never stays on a disabled control. A
  timeout (30 s), an error or a malformed answer is outcome unknown, a
  retry reuses the idempotency key, and closing while in flight shows
  sign.outcomeUnknown. The window comes from the window's own
  PatientContextDeps (contextDeps).
- SignButton and signingIdentityValid; createSimulatedAuthenticator
  (badge tap plus a 4-to-8-digit PIN for a co-sign, never stored; throws
  in a production build unless VITE_HMS_DEMO=1).
- CommandPalette: React Aria Autocomplete, TextField and Menu; nothing
  highlighted on open or on new results; a chosen item runs only after
  the palette has unmounted; Ctrl/Cmd+K by physical key, ignored while
  another modal holds the guard; search folding ي→ی, ك→ک, ه→ە for
  matching only; languageCommands.
- DensityControl (RadioGroup of RadioField and RadioButton),
  useDensityCommand and otherDensity. Stylesheets imported by
  styles.css; exports in the package entry point; README section.
- OQ-162 (open), DN-027 "default in use" bullet (description only, no
  decision), KA-043 (open; the authentication step has no catalogue
  text of its own).
