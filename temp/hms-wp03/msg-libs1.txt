feat(kernel): UUIDv7 ids, clinical and recorded time, money, TestClock

The first WP-0.3 kernel libraries (BUILD-PROMPT §5.3, §6.2, §8.1 P2 and
P10, §21 WP-0.3; docs/specs/modules/platform-kernel.md §5, §6, §8, §9),
built to the specification with no review (DN-040 decision 4):
- :platform:ids (hms.kernel.ids): RFC 9562 UUIDv7 generator on an
  injected Clock and RandomGenerator, monotonic within a millisecond and
  when the clock steps back; strict parsing; typed ids TenantId,
  FacilityId, PatientId, UserId.
- :platform:time (hms.kernel.time): RecordedTime only from the Clock,
  ClinicalTime with its original offset, ValidPeriod and ValidTime,
  AsOf, the SI-10 entry-time classifier (every row of the spec table at
  each tolerance and 1 us either side), safety parameters built only
  from resolved settings with synthetic values refused in TRAIN and
  PROD, BusinessCalendar with coverage reporting.
- :platform:money (hms.kernel.money): BigDecimal plus ISO 4217 currency,
  explicit rounding, exact allocation, cash rounding as configuration,
  effective-dated exchange rates. No floating point in the API.
- :platform:testing gains TestClock (forward, backward, shared zones).
- Gradle wiring for every WP-0.3 project; the projects not built yet
  are empty skeletons. spring-modulith-core joins the catalogue (Spring
  Modulith 2.1.1 line, already pinned) for the app-hms verification.

Kernel spec fixes found while building: a counter rollover at the last
48-bit millisecond throws; the strictness-direction property waits for
the tolerance definitions (DN-042). The specs' open items are numbered:
DN-042 to DN-044, OQ-170 to OQ-178. DN-041 is resolved by the lead.

Tests (Windows host, single-branch scratch clone holding exactly this
commit's content; not evidence of record): sh tools/ci/run fast
--no-evidence PASS, 8 of 8 steps, 1490 tests (699 Python, 277 backend,
514 frontend), 0 failures, secret scan clean.

Hazards: none changed in the hazard log. The time library implements
the kernel part of HAZ-0010 controls CTRL-0010-1 to -5 (SI-10) and
TestClock serves CTRL-0003-5 (SI-03 timing); all controls stay planned
until their module tests and records of record exist.
