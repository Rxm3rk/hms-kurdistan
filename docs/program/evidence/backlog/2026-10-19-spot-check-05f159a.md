## Method

For each row the source line was read straight from BUILD-PROMPT.md (`sed -n '<line>p' BUILD-PROMPT.md`), not from the tool's output, and compared cell by cell with the record in the committed `backlog.yaml`:
- ID, Feature, Size and Phase with `id`, `title`, `size` and `phase`, and the Appendix A section and group with `section` and `group`;
- Stance with `stance` and `domain_level` (the dagger);
- the Scope · Kurdistan note cell with `scope`, `kurdistan_note` (the italic part after the middle dot) and `placement_marked` ([P] in the note);
- `work_packages` with every mention of the ID in §21 (found with `awk` over lines 2267 to 2375, then read in context);
- `hazards` with the IDs of the hazard-log entries whose `features` list names the ID (read from `git show 05f159a:docs/safety/hazard-log.yaml` with PyYAML, and found with `grep` on the `features:` lines);
- the hand-edited fields with their defaults (`status: todo`; `tests` and `notes` empty).

The values the tool printed were then compared with the committed `backlog.yaml` by a throwaway script outside the repository: 110 fields (eleven for each of the ten rows) and the hand-edited fields, 0 differences.
