# Content sources

Published source documents for clinical instruments, placed here by a human (BUILD-PROMPT §3.5). The build implements a published instrument (NEWS2, PEWS, MEOWS, Braden, Morse, the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist, I-PASS) only from a source document a human has placed here with its licence status recorded. The agent never reproduces scoring tables, bands or codes from memory: until a source is present, the scoring engine ships with a synthetic table marked NOT FOR CLINICAL USE (`provenance: synthetic`) and a content request is logged in `docs/program/OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`. With the source present the build writes 100% boundary-value tests from it, and activation needs CSO approval [HUMAN]. To place a source, a human creates `docs/content-sources/<instrument>/` with the document (or, if its terms do not allow it in the repository, puts the file under `content/licensed/` and keeps only the record here) and a `SOURCE.md` record giving: title; publisher; edition or version; publication date; where it was obtained; SHA-256 of the file; licence or terms of use; licence status (`not-checked`, `free-to-use`, `licence-required`, `licence-held`); who placed it and when; and, for table-driven instruments such as PEWS and MEOWS, the variant the hospital chose, whose approval stays `requested` until a sign-off record exists. The Braden Scale needs a licence agreement and the I-PASS terms must be checked (§3.5). Work packages that need sources: WP-3.4 (`NUR-FLW-02` Braden and Morse), WP-3.5 (`CLN-CDS-05` NEWS2, PEWS, MEOWS), WP-3.7 (`SPC-OT-02` WHO checklist) and WP-3.11 (`CLN-HND-01` I-PASS).
